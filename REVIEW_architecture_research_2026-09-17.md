**KRSL → Kazakh: architecture audit and research priorities — 17 September 2026**

Recommendation: keep the keypoints-only constraint and the current ST-GCN → mT5 backbone for a corrected reference implementation. First repair evaluation and pose handling, then test skeleton–text alignment, learning-rate balance, and temporal compression. Sigma and SignDPO are the most relevant larger research directions. No defensible numerical BLEU improvement forecast is possible yet.

This review inspected the active trainer, Uni-Sign encoder, ASAN loader, preprocessing, metrics, evaluator, checkpoint conversion, experiment documents, and alternative encoder modules. It also read primary papers and Uni-Sign's official preprocessing implementation. Remote checks were read-only metadata inspection and small CPU probes; no training jobs or production code were changed. Alternative encoders were inspected for architectural relevance, not exhaustively tested. GPU inference on trained checkpoints was not performed.

**2. Pose representation: concrete defects and opportunities**

The active path is whole-body keypoints → shoulder scaling and despiking → bone offsets plus per-clip standardized coordinates → four part ST-GCNs → concatenation/projection → LayerNorm → mT5 encoder and autoregressive decoder. The model already has global temporal attention inside mT5; adding a transformer is not introducing its first long-range mechanism.

**Unused motion channels.** `map_keypoints_to_unisign_format` reads offsets `0:282`, absolute coordinates `282:564`, and confidence `1128:1410`. It never reads `564:1128`, where velocity and acceleration live. A CPU probe added 100 to every value in that block: the maximum difference in every mapped part was **0.0**. Temporal convolutions can still infer motion, but the advertised explicit motion features are not consumed. Either remove that unused bandwidth or introduce a separately gated motion branch. If enabling derivatives, pass actual sample timestamps: `compute_velocity` defaults to 50 fps, whereas sampled qazaqstantv data is 30 fps before 2× downsampling. Do not assume one fps for every source.

**Wrist and face topology.** `_map_coco_to_unisign_body` repeats elbow values into wrist slots 7/8; hand anchoring then reads those pseudo-wrist features. Actual wrists exist in wholebody indices 9/10 and the hand-root coordinates. Preserve them in a versioned input layout; compute wrist-relative bone offsets from real coordinates, not an already zero-rooted hand-offset channel. Neck construction also needs a geometrically explicit definition rather than interpreting averaged bone vectors as coordinates. The selected face nodes omit eyebrows; face confidence is averaged across the entire 88-point assembled group and broadcast to 18 nodes. Preserve per-node confidence, verify adjacency against anatomy, and test a compact eyebrow/eye/mouth representation. These are feature limitations, not proof that one larger face graph will win.

**Confidence saturates.** `enrich_keypoints` clips raw detector scores into [0,1]. Across 30 sampled clean qazaqstantv files, **0.998527494** of wholebody hand-joint scores were ≥1. This makes the hand confidence channel almost constant. Check detector score semantics and calibrate against labeled keypoint reliability; do not treat arbitrary min–max scaling or a sigmoid as calibrated confidence. Carry a separate detection-validity mask. The NPZ loader also ignores `person_found`; mask failed detections explicitly. Dedicated hand-detector arrays are available but unused: evaluate coordinate compatibility and tracking first, then compare wholebody hands with a confidence-aware dedicated-hand/fallback policy applied consistently across sources.

**Padding is not fully fixed.** Masking only before/after the entire temporal chain leaves three internal blocks free to create nonzero padded activations and mix them back into valid boundary frames. BatchNorm also counts padding in training statistics. A deterministic CPU probe (seed 123, random initialized encoder, eval mode, 12 real frames) compared the clip alone with the same clip padded to 24 frames: maximum valid-output difference **0.024163535**. This proves a padding-dependence defect; it does not measure trained-model accuracy impact. Propagate masks through every time-mixing block and use padding-aware normalization or test a per-frame normalization alternative. Plain GroupNorm over a tensor containing time is not automatically padding-independent. Add eval padding-invariance regression coverage and separate tests for training-statistic behavior.

**Pretrained tensor compatibility is not semantic compatibility.** Current weight loading checks names and shapes, then copies original x/y projection weights into bone-offset columns. That preserves a linear function only for equivalent inputs. Official Uni-Sign uses part-wise coordinate cropping/scaling and confidence masks; our coordinate meanings and facial node order differ. Reproduce its pose-only preprocessing as a controlled baseline before assuming transferred weights are ineffective. [Official Uni-Sign data code](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZechengLi19/Uni-Sign/main/datasets.py).

**Normalization and augmentation.** Current absolute standardization has separate x/y standard deviations across the clip's detected joints. It can distort aspect ratio and makes scale depend on motion extent. Compare against body-centered isotropic scaling plus local hand/face scaling, retaining global wrist/face anchors so local normalization does not erase signing location. Train with conservative coherent geometric noise and speed perturbations; recompute derived features after augmentation and keep evaluation deterministic. Do not indiscriminately mirror, reverse or crop signing while retaining the original transcript.

**Length audit lowered one suspected priority.** In deterministic samples of 500 train texts per source, token counts exceeded 128 for 0 informburo, 0 khabar and 1 qazaqstantv samples. Metadata showed no training clips above 2,000 raw frames in these three current annotation sets. Thus simply increasing length limits is not supported as a major current win. Still log truncation globally and preserve timestamps/duration in generated annotations; the loader's head truncation would otherwise remove evidence while retaining the transcript.

**3. Research that is most applicable**

The following results are authors' reported within-dataset comparisons, not predicted KRSL gains. Recent preprints require reproduction; they are not established guarantees.

| Primary source | Evidence / method | Application to our model |
|---|---|---|
| [Uni-Sign, ICLR 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.15187v3) | Part ST-GCNs and generative pretraining on 1,985 hours of CSL-News; includes a pose-only stage. | Keep a strong pose-only baseline and verify preprocessing before replacing its backbone. RGB stages are outside our scope. |
| [Pose-SLT ablations, 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.01532v1) | How2Sign BLEU-4: no normalization 0.73, frame-wise 1.13, SignSpace 2.17. Interpolating gaps ≤2 frames gives 2.43; ≤3 gives 2.33. Some strong rotations hurt. | Direct support for a normalization ablation and short-gap repair, not an exact reproduction claim for our modified dual-coordinate system. |
| [Unbiased Evaluation, CVIU / arXiv version](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13240) | Unified experiments retain some contrastive gains: CSL-Daily baseline 10.41±0.17 BLEU-4; CiCO-loss variant 12.03±0.06. Several reported gains shrink; lightweight translation alone does not win. | Prioritize reproducible alignment experiments and controlled optimization. The evidence does not say architecture never matters. |
| [Sigma, 2025 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.21223) | Skeleton-only part ST-GCN/mT5 framework with early sign–text fusion, hierarchical alignment, matching and language modeling. | Closest architectural match. Start with a simpler pose–text contrastive objective, then local alignment/matching; isolate each addition. |
| [Selective Contrastive Learning, 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.22374) | Selects video–text negatives using a reference model and an easy-to-hard curriculum. | Repeated broadcast stories create false negatives. | [SignDPO, 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.18034) | Skeleton-based preference learning over spatial, temporal and output-level perturbations. | [RVLF, 2025 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.07273) | Skeleton/RGB framework with GRPO and BLEU/ROUGE rewards; CSL-Daily GRPO gain over its SFT is 2.4 BLEU-4, distinct from total framework gains. | Transfer the sequence-level objective as a hypothesis; its visual backbone is not compatible with our modality constraint. Do not import RGB classifier weights into ST-GCN or assume signer diversity directly cures collapse. |

**4. Proposed architecture and training changes**

Proposed order of components, keeping keypoints as the sole inference modality:

```text
wholebody + reliable hand keypoints, timestamps, validity
  → body-centered global coordinates + locally normalized hand/face coordinates
  → pretrained part ST-GCNs with real wrists and block-level padding masks
  → optional gated motion residual (tested independently)
  → optional masked stride-2 temporal adapter
  → projection + LayerNorm
  → mT5 encoder → mT5 decoder → Kazakh text

training-only: pose–text global alignment → local alignment/matching
later: supervised CE + pose-conditioned preference optimization
```

This is a staged experimental design, not a claim that stacking all modules improves quality.

**Alignment first.** Add masked pooling and projection heads for pose and a separate text encoding pass; use a train-only contrastive loss jointly with CE. The text target must never enter the inference pose path. Freeze/cache a text teacher initially to reduce memory and moving targets. Validate Kazakh sentence retrieval before choosing that teacher. Batch 8 provides only seven in-batch negatives; gradient accumulation to 32 does not create 31 contrastive negatives. Use a carefully managed queue or cross-device gather, with same-story/duplicate negative masking. Then test local cluster or partial transport alignment if global retrieval improves but lexical accuracy stays weak.

**Learning-rate balance.** The current configuration updates the pretrained 582M mT5 at 5e-4 and the 5.35M encoder at 5e-5. This is a hypothesis to test, not automatically a bug. A first equal-update screen should compare current rates with mT5 5e-5 and encoder 1e-4, then add mT5 1e-4 / encoder 1e-4 if the lower decoder rate helps. Separately compare short decoder freezing or LoRA with full fine-tuning. Keep initialization, effective batch, metric protocol and data fixed. Monitor gradient norms and grounding rather than CE alone.

**Temporal efficiency.** Length bucketing can reduce wasted padding; sorting within an already formed batch does not reduce its maximum length. A stride-2 adapter may reduce mT5 encoder self-attention's quadratic component by roughly fourfold, not total model memory by fourfold. Preserve true output lengths and benchmark short/fast signs separately. Test a small multiscale temporal block before replacing ST-GCN with a completely new graph transformer. Do not combine the adapter and a larger decoder in the first ablation.

**Decoding.** The current four-beam search hardcodes repetition penalty 1.3 and prohibits repeated trigrams. Run a dev-only sweep of beam 1/4, repetition penalty 1.0/1.1/1.3, and trigram blocking off/on, using a bounded screen followed by full-dev verification. Track omissions, insertions, output lengths, numbers and names. Later consider compatible checkpoint averaging or a small ensemble and publish inference cost. Never tune decoding on test.

**Grounding diagnostics.** High uncentered cosine between mean-pooled clip features is not sufficient proof of representation collapse. Measure centered effective rank and retrieval, but put most weight on held-out changes in token NLL and generated content when pose is correctly paired versus shuffled across examples. Match length/source for shuffles; also compare static/reversed temporal inputs. A model that performs similarly under shuffled pose is not ready for a larger decoder or reward optimization. Cross-attention heatmaps are supporting evidence, not causal proof.

**Preference training.** After a grounded SFT baseline, compare ordinary output DPO, pose-conditioned DPO, and continued CE at matched budget. Generate preference candidates on training data only; ensure chosen text is more faithful rather than simply longer. If successful, consider GRPO or minimum-risk sequence training. Preserve content evaluation and human spot checks so optimizing n-gram rewards does not merely exploit the metric.

**5. Concrete experiment sequence**

| Stage | Work | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|---|
| E0 | Canonical splits, initializer provenance, one evaluator, representative selection IDs | No source-video overlap in any training stage; metric parity across entry points; full coverage or explicit failure |
| E1 | Re-score available checkpoints on valid dev; sweep decoding | Reproducible saved predictions and a clear baseline; no retraining required for decoder search |
| E2 | Padding repair and coordinate/topology parity tests | Padding-invariant eval outputs; correct wrist/face mappings; no change hidden behind identical tensor shapes |
| E3 | Current preprocessing vs upstream-compatible vs dual global/local normalization | Same data, initialization policy and update budget; broad per-source improvement |
| E4 | Learning-rate screen and conservative augmentation, separately | Stable grounding and full-dev improvement, not only lower train loss |
| E5 | CE baseline vs CE + global pose–text alignment | Better paired-vs-shuffled discrimination and translation; then consider local alignment |
| E6 | Optional temporal compression and motion residual, separately | Quality retained/improved at measured throughput and memory benefit |
| E7 | CE continuation vs DPO vs SignDPO-style post-training | Gains survive held-out full-dev evaluation and content checks |
| E8 | Three seeds for finalists; frozen test evaluation | Report mean/spread and paired bootstrap intervals clustered by source video |

Use the two GPUs for independent comparable candidates when memory is available, rather than requiring DDP for every experiment. Selection subsets should sample source videos and avoid domination by adjacent clips. Keep the test set sealed; inspect only metadata for leakage. A new split may need clean initialization or newly collected held-out broadcasts because of inherited checkpoint exposure.

Save manifests and checkpoint provenance as first-class artifacts. Checkpoints should retain resolved data/preprocessing configuration, model/tokenizer revisions, seed/RNG states, scheduler/optimizer/scaler state as applicable, and auxiliary vocabulary artifacts. The main trainer has no explicit seed control in the inspected file. Its partial accumulation flush is underweighted when fewer than four microbatches remain, and it advances the schedule after nonfinite-gradient skipped updates; these are reproducibility/optimization fixes, likely smaller than the primary findings. `evaluate_phase1.py` also hardcodes LoRA settings, which should come from checkpoint metadata.

My highest-confidence recommendation is to repair the measurement and representation contracts first. The strongest larger quality experiments are global/local skeleton–text alignment and pose-conditioned preference training. A larger language model, RGB fusion, prosody, GANs, or generic architecture replacement should not take priority under the current evidence and keypoints-only requirement.
